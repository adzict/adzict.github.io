Email Campaign Performance Analysis

Marketing analytics case study focused on email campaign performance, engagement metrics, and business recommendations.

Role: Junior Data Analyst

Tools: Excel / Google Sheets, formulas, pivot tables, charts

Project Type: Interview case study

Focus: Email marketing, campaign KPIs, performance comparison

Business Problem

The business needed to evaluate email campaign performance and understand which campaigns, audiences, or metrics were contributing to stronger or weaker results.

Goal

Analyze campaign data, identify performance patterns, and summarize actionable insights for campaign optimization.

What I Did

  • Cleaned and reviewed campaign performance data.
  • Compared key email marketing metrics across campaigns.
  • Identified stronger and weaker performing campaign patterns.
  • Prepared a concise performance analysis with recommendations.

Key Analysis / Visuals

  • Open rate / click rate comparison
  • Campaign performance ranking
  • Trend chart over time
  • Segment or audience comparison

Outcome / Recommendations

Provided a concise performance overview and recommended which campaign patterns should be repeated, improved, or tested further.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Excel
  • Marketing Analytics
  • KPI Reporting
  • Data Cleaning
  • Campaign Analysis
