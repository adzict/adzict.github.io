Customer Targeting with Predictive Analytics

Predictive analytics case study for identifying customers with a higher likelihood of taking a mortgage product.

Role: Data Analyst / Junior Data Scientist

Tools: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Matplotlib, Seaborn

Project Type: Interview case study

Focus: Customer targeting, classification, probability scoring, business recommendations

Business Problem

The business needed to identify potential customers who were likely to take out a mortgage, so relationship managers could focus outreach on the most promising leads.

Goal

Build a predictive model that scores potential customers by probability and provide a prioritized customer list for business follow-up.

What I Did

  • Performed exploratory data analysis on historical customer data.
  • Cleaned and transformed customer attributes for modeling.
  • Handled missing values and imbalanced target classes.
  • Tested multiple classification models and used XGBoost for probability prediction.
  • Created visual summaries for a non-technical business audience.

Key Analysis / Visuals

  • Target variable imbalance
  • Customer demographics
  • Income and balance distribution
  • Top customers by predicted probability

Outcome / Recommendations

Delivered a ranked list of high-potential customers and recommended targeted outreach, followed by campaign tracking to validate model performance in real business conditions.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Python
  • EDA
  • Data Preprocessing
  • Classification
  • Predictive Analytics
  • Stakeholder Presentation
